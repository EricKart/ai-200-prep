# 2-week study plan

> For people who already build on Azure and know containers, databases and messaging at a working level. About 3 hours a day.
> Track progress automatically at [aryantripathi.dev/ai-200](https://aryantripathi.dev/ai-200).

The rule for a short plan: **practise first, read second.** Do a module's practice questions, then read only the lesson sections for the questions you missed.

## Week 1

| Day | Do |
|---|---|
| 1 | Diagnostic on the site · LP1 (3 modules): practice → lesson gaps · [LP1 cheat sheet](../content/cheatsheets/lp1.md) |
| 2 | LP2 (4 modules), with extra time on M3 scaling and M4 dynamic sessions |
| 3 | LP3 (AKS, 3 modules) · one lab from LP1–LP3 |
| 4 | LP4 (Cosmos DB, 3 modules) |
| 5 | LP5 (PostgreSQL, 3 modules) |
| 6 | LP6 (Redis, 3 modules) · one lab from LP4–LP6 |
| 7 | **Mock exam 1** · review every wrong answer |

## Week 2

| Day | Do |
|---|---|
| 8 | LP7 M1 + M2 (Service Bus, Event Grid) |
| 9 | LP7 M3 + M4 + M5 (Functions, Durable Functions, Web PubSub) · LP7 lab |
| 10 | LP8 + LP9 (secrets, configuration, observability) · LP8 or LP9 lab |
| 11 | **Mock exam 2** · review |
| 12 | Weakest skill area: lessons + redo its wrong questions · [Which service?](../content/cheatsheets/which-service.md) |
| 13 | **Mock exam 3** · review |
| 14 | All cheat sheets · [exam-day guide](exam-day.md) |

**Book the exam when** every skill area shows ≥ 80% and you score 750+ on two mocks.
